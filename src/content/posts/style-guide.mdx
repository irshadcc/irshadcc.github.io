Posts live in `src/content/posts/` as `.md` or `.mdx` files. Plain Markdown covers most
needs; use `.mdx` when you want sidenotes.<Sidenote>Like this one. Sidenotes are numbered
automatically and sit in the right margin. On a phone, tap the number to expand it.</Sidenote>
Margin notes are the same thing without a number.<MarginNote>An unnumbered margin note.</MarginNote>

## Headings and text

Headings are set in Inter and body text in Source Serif 4, with
[links](https://astro.build) in the accent color.

### Math

Inline math like $y = Wx + b$ works with single dollars. Display math uses double dollars:

$$
\text{softmax}(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K} e^{z_j}}
$$
